How it works

A better first conversation with the U.S. market.

Ask a business question in your own words. The assistant helps you understand the landscape, identify next steps, and keep learning.

A practical starting point

Useful by design. Clear about its limits.

Use the assistant to get oriented—not to replace professional advice, official filings, or due diligence.

01 / WHAT IT DOES

It answers business questions and gives you a place to begin.

Responses may include practical explanations, source links when available, and follow-up questions that help narrow the topic.

02 / WHAT IT ISN’T

It is not a government service, law firm, accountant, or substitute for expert advice.

Always confirm important details with current official sources and qualified professionals.

What you can do

Bring the question you have today.

Get a stronger answer

The more context you share, the more useful the starting point.

Mention your country, industry, company stage, and what you are trying to decide. Even a sentence or two helps.
Ask for a high-level overview, a checklist of next steps, questions to take to an advisor, or sources to review.
If the first answer is too broad, ask the assistant to focus on one state, one business model, or one decision.
